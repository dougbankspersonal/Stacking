# Overview

You are competing with fellow titans of industry to build the tallest skyscrapers, in the city.

# Game Concepts

- Cards are _Floors_. _Floors_ have a top and bottom section, each with a number and color. When playing a _Floor_, rotate it so that the section you want to use is on the bottom: the top is ignored.
  - Each _Floor_ section has one of **3** colors, and a number **1-8**.
- A _Skyscraper_ is a set of played _Floors_ of matching color, where the _Floor_ numbers either strictly increase or decrease as you move to higher _Floors_.
- A _District_ is the set of _Skyscrapers_ in front of a given player.
  - Each player can only play cards into their own _District_.
  - A _District_ may have at most **5** _Skyscrapers_.

# Setup

- Deal **3** _Floors_ to each player.
- Place **2** _Floors_ from the deck face up next to the deck.
- Pick a start player.

# Gameplay

Each player, on their turn, will Play or Pass.

## Play

A player must play at least one _Floor_: they may play as many more as they'd like.
To play a _Floor_:

- Rotate so that the section you want to use is on the **bottom**.
- Place the _Floor_ in your _District_.
  - If it is not on top of an existing _Skyscraper_, it forms the first floor of a new _Skyscaper_.
    - You may have multiple _Skyscrapers_ of the same color.
    - You may not have more than **5** _Skyscrapers_ in your _District_.
  - If it is played on top of an existing _Skyscraper_:
    - It must match the color of the existing _Floors_ of the _Skyscraper_.
    - The _Floor_ numbers must strictly increase or decrease as you go up the _Skyscraper_.

After Play, a player draws until they have **3** _Floors_ in hand.

- They may draw from face-up _Floors_ or from the deck.
- Refill to **2** face-up _Floors_ afterwards if needed.

## Pass

If a player has no legal moves, they must show their _Floors_ to the other players and Pass.

Once a player has Passed their turn is skipped for the rest of the game.

Other players may continue taking turns.

## Wild Color Cards

Silver _Floors_ match any color.

## Initiative Tokens

Each player has tokens, each granting a one-time special power: players may spend the token to use the power during their turn.

- **Recycle:** Take a _Floor_ from the top of any _Skyscraper_ in your _District_ and return it to your hand.
- **Cycle 3:** Draw 3 _Floors_ and discard 3 _Floors_. Shuffle discarded _Floors_ back into the deck.
- **Foundation:** Add a _Floor_ to the **bottom** of a _Skyscraper_: resulting _Skyscraper_ must be valid.

# Game End

- If _Floor_ cards run out, the game ends immediately. This could be because:
  - A player wants to draw from the deck and it's empty.
  - There are not enough _Floors_ to refill the face-up row of _Floors_.
- If no players can play (everyone has passed), the game ends immediately.

# Scoring

For each Color (ignoring Silver), find the tallest _Skyscraper_ in your _District_. Earn 1 point for each shorter _Skyscraper_ of the same Color in any other District.
